## A. Academic Understanding: Holdout Regression Evaluation
- **Evaluating Continuous Prediction Quality:** Continuous models cannot be evaluated by "percent correct." Error must be measured by distance between predictions $\hat{y}$ and true scores $y$.
- **Metric Definitions & Dimensionality:**
  - $\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum |y_i - \hat{y}_i|$: Linear loss in original score units (points). Reflects median central error.
  - $\text{MSE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2$: Quadratic loss in squared units ($\text{points}^2$). Heavily penalizes large errors.
  - $\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\text{MSE}}$: Standard deviation of unexplained residuals in original score units.
  - $R^2 = 1 - \frac{\text{SS}_{\text{res}}}{\text{SS}_{\text{tot}}}$: Standardized percentage of target variance explained ($R^2 \le 1.0$).
- **The RMSE vs. MAE Gap:** Because $\text{RMSE} \ge \text{MAE}$, a small gap indicates that errors are consistently small without extreme outlier blunders.
