## 1. Academic Understanding: Univariate Feature Importance via ANOVA F-Regression

### Quantifying Factor Relevance in Educational Machine Learning
Understanding *which* student attributes are most predictive of examination outcomes is a core objective of educational data mining. Rather than treating machine learning as an uninterpretable "black box," univariate statistical testing provides transparent, objective factor ranking.

**ANOVA $F$-Regression (`f_regression`):**
Calculates the linear relationship between each preprocessed feature $x_j$ and the continuous target $y$ via univariate regression:
$$F = \frac{\text{MS}_{\text{model}}}{\text{MS}_{\text{residual}}} = \frac{r^2 / 1}{(1 - r^2) / (n - 2)}$$
A larger $F$-score combined with a statistically negligible $p$-value indicates a powerful linear association between the predictor and examination performance.

## 3. In-Project Significance & Viva Defense

### Why This Visualization Matters
This chart provides an evidence-based foundation for educational interventions and counselor recommendations. It demonstrates that observable student engagement (showing up to class and dedicating structured study time) has an effect size orders of magnitude greater than demographic or environmental attributes.

### Viva Defense Insight
- **Question:** *"Does a high F-score for Attendance prove that improving attendance will automatically raise a student's grade?"*
- **Defense Answer:** No. $F$-scores and correlation coefficients establish statistical association within historical observational data; they do not prove direct causality. Confounders like student motivation or foundational preparation influence both attendance and grades. However, it empirically highlights attendance as the highest-leverage focal area for academic counseling.
