## A. Academic Understanding: Univariate Hypothesis Testing with F-Regression
- **Testing Linear Significance:** Computes Pearson correlation $r$ between each feature and continuous `Exam_Score`, then transforms it into an $F$-statistic: $F = \frac{r^2}{1-r^2}(n-2) \sim \mathcal{F}(1, n-2)$.
- **Degrees of Freedom:** With $n=5,285$ training students, degrees of freedom are $(1, 5283)$. A critical $F$-value at $\alpha=0.001$ is only $\approx 10.83$.
