# Companion Explanation: AcademiQ_Clustering_Evaluation.csv

## A. Academic Understanding: Clustering Validation & Model Selection
- **The Unsupervised Evaluation Challenge:** Without ground truth labels, clustering algorithms cannot compute accuracy. Internal geometric criteria must be used.
- **Inertia vs. Silhouette Score:**
  - *Inertia (Within-Cluster Sum of Squares):* Monotonically decreases as $K$ increases; reaches 0 at $K=n$. Cannot select $K$ alone without penalization.
  - *Silhouette Score ($s \in [-1, 1]$):* Measures if points are closer to their own cluster than neighboring clusters. Peaks at optimal cluster separation.
