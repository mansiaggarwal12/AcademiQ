## A. Academic Understanding: Latent Orthogonal Coordinate Projections
- **Matrix Projection into Latent Space:** When a high-dimensional design matrix $\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{n 	imes p}$ is multiplied by the truncated eigenvector matrix $\mathbf{V}_d \in \mathbb{R}^{p 	imes d}$, the resulting matrix $\mathbf{Z} = \mathbf{X} \mathbf{V}_d \in \mathbb{R}^{n 	imes d}$ represents coordinates in a lower-dimensional orthogonal space.
- **Decorrelation:** The columns of $\mathbf{Z}$ have zero covariance ($	ext{Cov}(Z_j, Z_k) = 0$ for $j 
e k$), eliminating multicollinearity completely.
